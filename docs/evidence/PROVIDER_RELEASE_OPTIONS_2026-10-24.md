## Cost conclusions and uncertainty

- AWS's existing USD 5 Budget covers AWS, **not** a separately billed provider.
  A paid market-data plan, add-on, exchange fee or annual commitment needs its
  own bounded decision. No such fee was incurred or commitment made here.
- Twelve Data's selected current card, residual From-$149 wording and older
  support pricing are not a single verified minimum quote. Do not treat $149
  as a guaranteed current entitlement/price. The official Markdown link could
  not be read by the web tool (unsupported content type); no fallback source or
  account checkout was used to manufacture a price confirmation.
- Marketstack's paid per-request price is not a monthly spending cap. Free-plan
  hard-stop/automatic-upgrade behavior and the exact applicable order terms were
  not verified. Do not add a payment method or enable overages based on this table.
- None of these three alternatives was established as a free, fully licensed,
  five-symbol public replacement in this bounded review. This does **not** prove
  that no suitable provider or individually negotiated permission exists.

Recommended next decision: seek written scope/fee confirmation for the current
provider before paying for a migration. Specify audience, no downloads/raw API
redistribution, exact five assets, EOD/latest-available versus delayed data,
short caching and AI-derived displayed summaries. No inquiry was sent. Do not
quietly redefine the finished live product as a hosted fixture demonstration.
Keep the existing API/display/hosting and publication gates while undecided.
