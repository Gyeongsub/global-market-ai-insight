## Sources and confirmed page facts

The [official individual pricing page](https://twelvedata.com/pricing) still
lists Basic as free, 8 API credits/minute and 800/day, with internal non-display
usage. Credits are endpoint-weighted, not an unlimited request allowance.
Public pricing does not prove this account's current billing settings, nor
guarantee perpetual free use or grant external display rights.

The [official use guide](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5332349-commercial-and-personal-usage),
dated August 4, 2026, places individual plans in personal/internal use, including
non-production educational/development work. It excludes redistribution and
commercial third-party display; business use still has exchange conditions.
Endpoint availability or metadata listings do not establish entitlement.

The [Terms of Use](https://twelvedata.com/terms), updated January 1, 2026,
sections 1/2/3 distinguish internal use, non-display, third-party distribution,
and derived data that cannot recreate the underlying data. External display
needs an expressly permitted subscription/add-on or written agreement. Section
2 also restricts retention to permitted timeframes. Generic fee provisions in
section 5 do not establish this Basic account's actual overage configuration.
No assumption that a UI delay or an application TTL creates a data license.

The [attribution guide](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/12647398-attribution-guidelines-for-using-twelve-data),
dated January 12, 2026, specifies visible attribution and a provider link for
external content unless an agreement changes that requirement; private uses
are treated separately. Attribution is an obligation, not permission to publish.
